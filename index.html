Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I wear?

Smart casual. We want you to feel comfortable, so wear whatever makes you feel your best and lets you celebrate and dance. The ceremony and reception are both located at The Talbot Inn, which will have heating. However, as it is the middle of winter, it may still be a bit chilly.

Are children invited?

While we love all the little ones in our lives, our wedding will be an adults only celebration. We are only able to accommodate children within our immediate family. We hope you understand and can still join us to celebrate!

Can I bring a plus-one?

Please refer to your invitation or contact us if you are unsure.

Is the venue accessible?

The ceremony and celebration has step-free access, accessible parking and accessible facilities.

Can I leave my car overnight?

Parking at the Talbot Inn is charged at £10 for overnight guests which is payable on arrival at reception. Alternative complimentary parking can be found across the road.

Anthing not to bring?

We kindly ask that you don't bring or throw confetti at the venue, as its use incurs a £300 charge. Thank you for understanding and helping us avoid the additional cost.

Who should I contact on the day?

Please contact Krystyna at The Talbot Inn on 0044 1483 225188 and she should be able to contact Natalie or Shona if they do not have their phones to hand.
